About Me

I'm a Computer Systems Engineering student at UET Peshawar, building practical, hands-on expertise in networking and Linux system administration. My interest in this field grew out of taking networks apart to understand how they actually work — from IP addressing and routing to the Linux systems that keep infrastructure running.

My current focus areas include network infrastructure, routing and switching, Linux administration, network troubleshooting, and the fundamentals of network security. I'm also developing early skills in automation and cloud infrastructure, since I see these as the natural next step for a modern network engineer.

To be direct about where I stand: I'm not a working professional yet. What I have is consistent, hands-on lab and coursework experience — configuring routers and switches in Cisco Packet Tracer, administering Ubuntu and WSL systems, and working through real troubleshooting scenarios. My near-term goal is an internship or entry-level role where I can apply and grow these skills.
